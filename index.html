🚩 4 Golden Rules to Spot Phishing

Always pause and verify before entering passwords or downloading attachments.

1. Artificial Urgency

"Your account will be suspended in 2 hours!" Attackers create panic so you act without thinking.

2. Lookalike Domains

Check spelling carefully: amaz0n-support.com or paypaI.com (using a capital I) are fake.

3. Generic Greetings

Messages addressed to "Dear Valued Customer" instead of your real name are often mass phishing blasts.

4. Unexpected Attachments

Never open unexpected .zip, .exe, or macro-enabled invoices from unknown senders.
